# Exercise 5: Improve and present the workflow idea

<ExerciseHeaderImage :exercise="5" />

<CourseProgress :current="5" />

Turn the physical Flow-card idea into a free-form flipchart map. Use a photo and your preferred image-capable Generative AI tool as a second reader, improve the map with human judgement, complete the Leadership Handover Card, and present the idea.

**Time:** 14:45–15:57, 72 minutes.

> **Access:** Use any image-capable Generative AI tool available to your group, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Copilot. No Power Automate build or exact product configuration is required.

## Preparation

- Bring the physical Flow-card arrangement from Exercise 4.
- Prepare one clean flipchart sheet, thick pens, Worksheet 6C, and one internet-connected device per group where possible.
- Choose a photographer, a person who enters the prompt, and a timekeeper.
- Use only fictional or sanitised workflow information.
- Before taking the photo, remove names, email addresses, customer details, personal data, confidential information, real reference numbers, faces, badges, and unrelated flipcharts from the frame.
- If no suitable tool or image upload is available, use the facilitator-led review questions in Practice 3.

> **Important:** Generative AI provides review ideas. It cannot confirm Power Automate feasibility, licences, connectors, permissions, compliance, or production readiness. Your group decides what fits the situation and records questions that still need confirmation.

## Practice 1: Draw the flow map (10 minutes) {#practice-1}

**Primary target:** Turn the physical card arrangement into a free-form workflow map that another person can follow.

**Learner output:** One flipchart map showing the proposed start, sequence, people, decisions, exceptions, and intended outcome.

1. Use the physical cards as inspiration and draw the workflow on the flipchart.
2. Show where the work starts and the outcome the group wants.
3. Add the important Actions, people, human decisions, and any Condition or exception that helps explain the idea.
4. Use arrows, words, symbols, colours, or sketches that make sense to the group.
5. Add a question mark beside anything that still needs confirmation.

The drawing does not need to reproduce exact Power Automate Trigger or Action names. The goal is to communicate the business idea clearly.

### Checkpoint

A participant who did not draw the map can explain how the work starts, what happens, where people decide, and how it ends.

## Practice 2: Take a safe photo (5 minutes) {#practice-2}

**Primary target:** Create a clear, safe image that shows only the workflow map intended for review.

**Learner output:** One readable, tightly cropped photo with no prohibited information or identifiable people.

1. Check the map for names, email addresses, customer information, personal data, confidential details, and real operational reference numbers. Cover or remove anything that should not be uploaded.
2. Move people, badges, other tables, and unrelated flipcharts out of the frame.
3. Take the photo straight on in good light.
4. Crop the image to the map and check that the labels can be read.
5. Ask one other group member to complete the safety check before upload.

### Checkpoint

The photo contains only the sanitised workflow map and is clear enough for another person to review.

## Practice 3: Review and revise with Generative AI (15 minutes) {#practice-3}

**Primary target:** Use Generative AI feedback as input for a human-led revision of the flow map.

**Learner output:** An improved flipchart map plus useful feedback and unresolved questions to carry into Worksheet 6C.

1. Open any image-capable Generative AI tool available to the group.
2. Upload the safe photo and paste the prompt below.

   ```text
   You are reviewing a conceptual workflow map created during a leadership workshop.

   The attached photo is the main source. Read the labels as carefully as you can. If any part is unclear, say what you cannot read or understand instead of inventing missing information.

   Goal:
   Help our group make the workflow idea clearer, more complete, and easier to explain.

   Review the map and:
   - briefly restate how you understand the flow;
   - identify unclear or missing steps;
   - point out missing owners, human decisions, or exception routes;
   - suggest practical improvements or simplifications; and
   - list questions that a process owner or IT team still needs to confirm.

   This is a high-level business map. It does not need exact Power Automate Trigger or Action names. Do not claim that the idea is technically feasible, compliant, licensed, or ready to deploy.

   Keep the response concise and organise it with clear headings.
   ```

3. Read the response together. Compare it with the physical cards, the selected opportunity, and what the group actually means.
4. Choose the advice that helps. Ignore or correct advice that does not fit the situation.
5. Revise the flipchart map freely. Keep question marks beside matters that still need confirmation.

**Facilitator-led fallback:** If the group cannot upload the photo, ask: What is unclear? What step or owner may be missing? Where must a person decide? What exception could stop the work? What must the process owner or IT confirm?

### Checkpoint

The revised map is clearer than the first draft, and the group can distinguish its own decisions from Generative AI suggestions.

## Practice 4: Complete the handover and prepare (12 minutes) {#practice-4}

**Primary target:** Turn the improved map into a concise leadership handover and presentation structure.

**Learner output:** A completed Worksheet 6C and three agreed presentation points.

1. Complete [Worksheet 6C: Leadership Handover Card](/resources/worksheets#_6c-leadership-handover-card).
2. Record useful Generative AI feedback, changes made to the map, and questions that still need confirmation.
3. Confirm the process owner, expected benefit, measurement approach, people affected, support needs, next decision, responsible role, and follow-up date.
4. Prepare three presentation points:
   1. the problem and expected benefit;
   2. how the improved flow works and where people remain responsible; and
   3. the next decision, owner, and most important unresolved question.
5. Choose speakers and keep the improved map and Handover Card visible as prompts. Do not read the worksheet word for word.

### Checkpoint

The group can explain the idea in three minutes and identify the next responsible decision without claiming that the flow is ready to build.

## Practice 5: Present and receive feedback (30 minutes) {#practice-5}

**Primary target:** Communicate the automation idea and use facilitator feedback to clarify its next step.

**Learner output:** One five-minute group round with a clear idea, handover, and recorded facilitator feedback.

Each of the six groups has five minutes:

- **3 minutes:** Present the improved flow map and the three agreed presentation points.
- **2 minutes:** Receive facilitator feedback on business clarity, ownership, people, exceptions, and the next decision.

Record the most useful facilitator feedback on Worksheet 6C. The facilitator is reviewing the quality of the proposal, not approving a technical solution.

### Checkpoint

All six groups have presented, received feedback, and recorded one useful follow-up.

## If you get stuck

- If the AI cannot read the photo, take a clearer cropped photo or type only the unreadable labels as additional context.
- If the advice is broad, ask the tool to refer specifically to what is visible in the map.
- If the advice conflicts with the group's situation, explain why and do not use it.
- Keep technical, licence, policy, and permission questions as matters for the responsible process owner or IT team.

## Summary

The improved map and Leadership Handover Card support the next business discussion. They do not prove that the workflow will work in a real tenant. Complete Worksheet 7 at 15:57–16:00.
